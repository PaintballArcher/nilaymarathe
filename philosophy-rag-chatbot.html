Overview

This project developed a retrieval-augmented generation chatbot for context-aware questions about Indian philosophy texts. I curated a domain-specific corpus from Indian texts, including scanned handwritten material.

Gemini AI was used for optical character recognition on the scans before the corpus was prepared as input to the language model.

01 / CONTRIBUTIONS

What I worked on

  • Developed a RAG-based language model for context-aware querying of Indian texts.
  • Curated a domain-specific text corpus.
  • Applied OCR to old scanned handwritten texts using Gemini AI.
