Overview

CitySimRL brings highway, merge, roundabout, and intersection scenarios together in one autonomous driving simulation. The goal was to evaluate model-free and model-based reinforcement learning approaches as traffic density changes.

I used the HighwayEnv framework and engineered custom lane network transitions so the driving agent could be evaluated continuously across different road environments.

01 / CONTRIBUTIONS

What I worked on

  • Stitched multiple traffic scenarios into one composite 2D simulation.
  • Designed lane transitions between heterogeneous road networks.
  • Used Intelligent Driver Model traffic dynamics around the agent-controlled vehicle.
