AutoDock for power-assist wheelchairs.

Helping a power-assist wheel navigate back to its wheelchair through localization and path planning.

Overview

AutoDock is a concept for autonomously moving a power-assist wheel toward a wheelchair. It combines relative signal strength, wheelchair pose estimation, and path planning on a scanned map of the environment.

I engineered a signal-strength-based algorithm in MATLAB to estimate distance from the wheel's home position and used A* and Dijkstra algorithms to generate navigation paths.

01 / CONTRIBUTIONS

What I worked on

  • Estimated distance using relative signal strength and captured wheelchair pose.
  • Generated navigation paths with A* and Dijkstra based on a scanned map.
  • Connected localization and path planning into an autonomous docking concept.
02 / RESULT

Pose estimation

The project reported wheelchair pose estimation with a 2% error over distances up to 25 metres.
